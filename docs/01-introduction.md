# 1. Introduction

This document constitutes the second stage of the network implementation project carried out for the Centre de divertissement de l'Estrie, as part of the Network Implementation Project course. Following the project definition submitted at the previous stage, this stage aims to prepare the complete planning of the work before beginning the implementation. Good planning is essential: it establishes who does what, in what order, at what cost and on what schedule, while anticipating the risks likely to hinder the success of the project.

The document successively presents the team's management plan, a revision of the organization's context, the functional specification, the risk analysis (FMECA), the task list in the form of a work breakdown structure, the detailed budget comparing the internal cost and the price billed to the client, the updated budget, whose tracking structure is in place for the implementation, then the initial planning (schedule, PERT chart, Gantt chart and S-curve). The document ends with the network log, which records the complete configuration of the network, and with the conclusion.

The quantified elements such as the task list, the budget, the FMECA table, as well as the planning data and charts (Gantt and S-curve), are grouped in an attached Excel workbook (Planification_calculs.xlsx). The hourly rates and durations are configurable there and everything recalculates automatically, which facilitates the weekly update during the implementation. This report sets out their structure and analysis, and refers to the workbook for the numerical details.
