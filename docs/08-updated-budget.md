# 8. Updated Budget

The updated budget (or budget to date) is the expression of the changes that occur during the course of the project. At the start of the implementation, it is identical to the initial budget presented in [section 7](07-detailed-budget.md); it will then be revised every week by the acting project manager to take into account minor contingencies internal to a package, changes affecting several packages and, where applicable, cost overruns at the project level.

Weekly tracking is kept in the “Budget à jour” (updated budget) tab of the Planification_calculs.xlsx workbook, based on the rates, durations and costs of the Budget tab. Each week, the acting project manager enters the hours actually worked per package, the supplies actually purchased and, if applicable, the approved budget changes. From these, the workbook derives the updated budget of each package, the actual cost to date, the remaining amount to commit, the forecast at completion and the variance from the budget.

Changes are classified into three levels. A minor contingency internal to a package is absorbed by that package's budget and simply recorded. A change affecting several packages results in a budget transfer between the packages concerned, with no effect on the total. A project-level cost overrun is charged to the affected package and drawn from the 10 % contingency reserve; if it exceeds the remaining reserve, the total budget increases by the same amount and the variance is reported in that week's meeting report.

Progress is also tracked in hours: the cumulative actual hours are compared, week after week, with the planned hours of the initial S-curve ([section 9.4](09-initial-planning.md)), which makes it possible to detect a delay before it turns into a cost overrun. The tab includes a chart comparing the planned and actual cumulative hours, as well as a dated log of the approved budget changes, with their level and their approver.
