# 7. Detailed Budget

The detailed budget quantifies the cost of the project. A distinction is made between the personnel cost for the company (actual internal cost) and the price billed to the client. The internal cost is based on a rate of $25/h for execution and $30/h for management; the client, for their part, is billed at a single rate of $45/h, which covers the company's overhead and margin. A 10 % contingency reserve for technical contingencies is added, in accordance with the initial budget approach.

## 7.1 Personnel Cost for the Company (Internal Cost)

The internal cost consists of labour (execution at $25/h, management at $30/h), supplies and a 10 % contingency reserve. The breakdown by package and the totals are calculated in the Excel workbook.

The breakdown of the internal cost by package (hours, labour, supplies and total) and the calculation of the contingency reserve are found in the “Budget” tab of the Planification_calculs.xlsx workbook, submitted with this report.

## 7.2 Personnel Cost for the Client

The priced offer presented to the client applies a uniform rate of $45/h to all hours (execution and management), to which the supplies and a contingency reserve are added. This rate includes the company's margin.

## 7.3 Budget Summary

The summary reconciles the internal cost with the price offered to the client and derives the project's gross margin from them. These indicators (internal cost, client price, labour variance, and gross margin in dollars and as a percentage) are calculated in the Excel workbook.
